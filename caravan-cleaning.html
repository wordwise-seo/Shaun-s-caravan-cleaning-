A caravan picks up grime in ways a car never does. It sits still for months at a time, often under trees or open sky, so algae, black streaks and green film build up across the roof and down the sides. Our mobile caravan cleaning service deals with all of it in one visit — wherever your caravan is kept.

What caravan cleaning covers

Every caravan is different, so we tailor the work to yours. The core jobs we get asked for most are:

  • Exterior cleaning — a safe, low-pressure wash of the full body, sills and edges.
  • Roof cleaning — where most grime and streaking actually starts.
  • Window cleaning — acrylic windows cleaned without scratching or hazing.
  • Awning cleaning — fabric and poles brought back, mould and mildew treated.
  • Black streak removal — the number-one caravan complaint, lifted and protected.

Why not just use a car wash or jet wash?

Pressure washers and acidic cleaners are the fastest way to damage a caravan. High pressure forces water past seals and into seams; acid attacks gelcoat, decals and trims. We use caravan-specific products, soft mitts and a low-pressure rinse — slower, but it protects your investment and gives a finish that lasts.

We come to you, across Surrey & Sussex

We're fully mobile and self-sufficient, carrying our own purified water and power. That means we can clean your caravan at home, at work, or on a storage site with no facilities. You don't need to be there — as long as we can reach the vehicle and lockers, we'll get on with it.

Common questions

How much does caravan cleaning cost?
Exterior cleans start from £135 and a complete interior-and-exterior valet from £225, for caravans up to 7.5m. The price is always confirmed before we start.
Do you clean caravans on storage sites?
Yes. We carry purified water and our own power, so we can clean your caravan on any storage site, with or without facilities.
How often should a caravan be cleaned?
Most owners book a thorough clean twice a year — before the season and before winter storage — with the odd top-up after a long trip.
