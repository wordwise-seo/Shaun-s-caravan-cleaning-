How to Clean a Caravan: A Step-by-Step Guide

Cleaning a caravan well is mostly about order and the right products. Work top to bottom, never let cleaner dry on the surface, and keep pressure low. Here's the routine we use.

1. Start with the roof

The roof sheds grime down the sides, so clean it first — safely, from access equipment rather than leaning a ladder on soft panels.

2. Pre-rinse the body

A gentle rinse loosens loose dirt so you're not grinding it into the gelcoat.

3. Wash top to bottom

Use a caravan-specific shampoo and a soft mitt, one panel at a time, rinsing as you go.

4. Treat black streaks

Apply a dedicated streak remover to the usual runs under windows and lockers.

5. Windows, wheels and finish

Clean acrylic windows with the right product, dress the wheels, then dry with a soft towel to avoid watermarks.
