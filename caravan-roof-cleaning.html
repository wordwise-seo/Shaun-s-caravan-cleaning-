Caravan Roof Cleaning

The roof is where almost every caravan problem begins. We clean it safely and thoroughly, removing the moss and algae that cause black streaks down your sides.

Get a quoteCall 01306 000 000

Most owners never see their caravan roof — which is exactly why it's the most neglected and most important area to clean. Rainwater carries roof grime down the sides, and that's what creates those stubborn black streaks. Clean the roof properly and you tackle the cause, not just the symptom.

How we do it safely

We work from proper access equipment, never by leaning ladders against soft panels. The roof is cleaned with caravan-safe products and a soft brush, skylights and vents worked around carefully, then everything rinsed at low pressure.

Why it matters

  • Stops black streaks at the source
  • Protects roof seams and seals from organic growth
  • Keeps your caravan looking cared-for and holding value

Common questions

Is it safe to walk on my caravan roof?
We don't rely on walking the roof — we use access equipment and reach techniques to clean it safely without stressing the panels.
How often should the roof be cleaned?
At least once a year. Caravans stored under trees benefit from twice-yearly roof cleans.
