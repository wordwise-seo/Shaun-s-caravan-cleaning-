How to Remove Black Streaks From a Caravan

Black streaks are caused by rainwater carrying roof grime and oxidised gelcoat down the sides. Scrubbing harder isn't the answer — the right product and a clean roof are.

Clean the roof first

Leave the roof dirty and the streaks return within weeks. Always start there.

Use a dedicated streak remover

Apply it to the runs under windows and lockers, work it by hand, and rinse.

Seal the surface

A sealant makes water bead off, dramatically slowing how fast streaks return.
