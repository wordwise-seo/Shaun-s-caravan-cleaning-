Caravan Cleaning Services

Caravan Exterior Cleaning

A safe, thorough wash of your caravan's full exterior — body, sills, edges and wheels — using caravan-specific products and gentle technique.

Get a quoteCall 01306 000 000

The exterior takes the brunt of the weather. Our exterior clean works methodically from the roof down: loosening dirt, algae and black streaks with the right products, agitating by hand with soft mitts, then rinsing with low pressure so nothing is forced past the seals.

What's included

  • Full body washed top to bottom
  • Sills and edges hand-worked to lift staining
  • Wheels cleaned and tyres dressed
  • Windows cleaned of watermarks
  • Protective rinse additive applied

For longer-lasting protection, add a hand wax or gelcoat sealant — ask about our Premium Exterior option.

Common questions

How long does an exterior clean take?
Around 4–5 hours for a caravan up to 7.5m, depending on condition.
Will it remove black streaks?
An exterior clean treats light streaking; heavy staining is best tackled with our dedicated black-streak removal.
