Caravan Window Cleaning

Caravan windows are acrylic, not glass — and they scratch easily. We clean them the right way, leaving them clear and smear-free.

Get a quoteCall 01306 000 000

Reach for a household glass cleaner or a gritty cloth and you'll haze or scratch acrylic windows permanently. We use products and cloths made for acrylic, lifting dirt gently and finishing clear.

Included

  • Exterior and interior window cleaning
  • Light scratch and haze reduction where possible
  • Seals wiped and treated to stay supple

Common questions

Can you remove scratches from acrylic windows?
We can reduce light surface scratching and fogging; deep scratches may need specialist polishing, which we'll advise on.
What shouldn't I use on caravan windows?
Avoid glass cleaners with ammonia and any abrasive cloth — both damage acrylic. See our guide on cleaning windows without scratching.
