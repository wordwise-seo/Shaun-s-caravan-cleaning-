Caravan Black Streak Removal

Black streaks are the most common — and most hated — caravan problem. We lift them from the gelcoat and protect the surface so they take longer to return.

Get a quoteCall 01306 000 000

Those dark runs beneath windows, lockers and along the roof line are caused by rainwater carrying roof grime and oxidised gelcoat down the sides. Scrubbing harder won't fix it. We use dedicated black-streak removers worked in by hand, then seal the surface so water beads off instead of staining.

Our process

  • Roof cleaned first — the source of the streaks
  • Streak remover applied and worked by hand
  • Surface rinsed, dried and protected with sealant

Pair it with roof cleaning for the longest-lasting result.

Common questions

Why do black streaks keep coming back?
Because the roof is still shedding grime. We always recommend cleaning the roof alongside streak removal to break the cycle.
Can all black streaks be removed?
The vast majority lift cleanly. Very old streaks on heavily oxidised gelcoat may need machine compounding, which our Restoration Valet covers.
