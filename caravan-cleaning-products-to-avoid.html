Caravan Cleaning Products to Avoid

Some of the most common cleaning kit is exactly what you shouldn't use on a caravan.

Avoid acidic wheel cleaners

They attack gelcoat and trims. Use a non-acid alternative.

Avoid ammonia glass cleaners

They haze acrylic windows. Use an acrylic-safe product.

Avoid high-pressure washers

They force water past seals and into seams. Low pressure only.

Avoid abrasive pads

They scratch gelcoat and acrylic alike. Soft mitts and microfibre only.
