Caravan Awning Cleaning

Awnings trap damp, mould and mildew — especially if packed away less than bone-dry. We clean fabric and poles and treat the green spots.

Common questions

Can mould be fully removed from an awning?
We remove most mould staining, but long-set black mould can leave a shadow in the fabric. We'll always be honest about what's achievable.
Should I clean my awning before storing it?
Always store an awning clean and bone-dry — it's the single best way to prevent mould.
