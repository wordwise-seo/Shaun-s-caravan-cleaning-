A caravan valet goes beyond a wash. It's a complete refresh of the vehicle inside and out — bodywork, roof and windows on the outside; upholstery, carpets, kitchen and washroom on the inside. The result is a caravan that smells fresh and looks like it's just come out of the showroom.

Which valet do you need?

  • Mini valet — a quick refresh between trips: exterior tidy and interior vacuum and wipe-down.
  • Full valet — the works, inside and out, with black streaks treated and surfaces protected.
  • Annual maintenance valet — a scheduled clean that keeps on top of grime and protects resale value.

Valeting before a sale

First impressions sell caravans. A full valet means clean photos, a fresh-smelling interior and no off-putting black streaks — the difference between a quick sale and weeks of viewings.

Mobile, at your convenience

As with all our work, valeting is fully mobile across Surrey and Sussex. We bring everything needed and work at your home or storage site, with no deposit to book.

Common questions

What's the difference between cleaning and valeting?
Cleaning focuses on getting the caravan clean; valeting is a fuller treatment that also protects surfaces and refreshes the interior.
How much is a full caravan valet?
A complete interior-and-exterior valet starts from £225 for caravans up to 7.5m, confirmed before we begin.
Can I set up regular valets?
Yes — an annual maintenance valet keeps your caravan on top of grime and is the easiest way to protect its condition and value.
